FROM CONCEPT TO MARKET

Protecting innovation is only part of the journey.

Successful product development involves more than protection. Clearance matters too. RIDAN IP helps businesses identify valuable features, assess relevant third-party rights, select appropriate forms of protection and revisit decisions as products develop.

01

Shape the invention and identify the features that may create value.

Innovation often begins before any patent application is filed. Valuable intellectual property is often found in technical features, product architecture, manufacturing methods, software functionality or design decisions that create a competitive advantage.

RIDAN IP helps businesses identify what is genuinely innovative, distinguish commercial priorities from technical detail and determine where intellectual property protection may support broader business objectives.

02

Assess relevant third-party rights before committing to protection or investment.

A strong innovation strategy requires an understanding of the surrounding intellectual property landscape. Existing patents, trade marks, registered designs and other rights may affect product development, manufacturing or market entry.

RIDAN IP supports clearance and freedom-to-operate assessments to help businesses make informed decisions, reduce risk and identify potential obstacles before significant investment is made.

03

File a patent application to establish an early filing date.

Securing an early filing date can be critical. Patent applications should be prepared carefully to support both immediate protection and future commercial objectives such as investment, licensing or international expansion.

RIDAN IP prepares and files patent applications designed to protect valuable innovation while providing a platform for future prosecution and portfolio development.

04

Progress the application through patent office search and examination towards grant.

Most patent applications require ongoing interaction with patent offices. Search reports, examination objections and procedural requirements must be managed effectively to maximise the prospects of obtaining meaningful protection.

RIDAN IP advises throughout prosecution, helping businesses navigate examination proceedings and develop strategies that align with commercial objectives.

05

Maintain, licence, defend and enforce secured patent rights as commercial opportunities arise.

Intellectual property often becomes most valuable after protection has been secured. Patents may support licensing discussions, investment activity, collaboration arrangements, market positioning or enforcement against competitors.

RIDAN IP provides ongoing support to help businesses manage patent portfolios, extract commercial value from intellectual property assets and respond when disputes or opportunities arise.
