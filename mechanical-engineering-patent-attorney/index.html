FROM DEVELOPMENT TO MARKET

Patent support shaped around the product.

A new mechanism, a manufacturing improvement or a change to product architecture can raise several IP questions at once. What is worth protecting? When should you file? Could another business's rights affect the launch?

RIDAN IP helps engineering businesses and product-development consultancies consider those questions together. Support can begin with an early concept, drawings or a prototype and continue through patent examination, product changes and portfolio decisions.

Identify inventions and set priorities

Invention discussions and patentability or prior-art assessments help identify the technical contribution and decide where further work is justified. Filing priorities should reflect development plans, disclosure dates, target markets and the available budget.

Draft and prosecute patent applications

Original patent drafting should explain how the invention works, identify the broader inventive concept and preserve useful fall-back positions. RIDAN IP handles UK and European filing and prosecution, with international filing strategy and coordination with overseas associates where required.

Assess clearance and protect appearance

Freedom-to-operate and product-clearance advice considers relevant third-party rights and practical options before important product decisions. Separately, registered designs may protect a product's appearance. Patent protection, design protection and trade secrets can form different parts of the same strategy.

DEVELOPING THE INVENTION

Why technical understanding matters.

For a mechanical invention, the useful patentable idea may lie in the relationship between components rather than in any one part. Geometry, movement, force paths, fluid flow, thermal behaviour and control can affect how the invention should be described and claimed.

This matters when a product is still changing. The first patent application should be considered alongside development, disclosure and commercial plans, so that the technical disclosure supports the protection the business may later need.
