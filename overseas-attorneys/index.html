YOUR EUROPEAN ASSOCIATE

Personal service. Professional trust.

RIDAN IP supports overseas patent attorneys and agents with their clients’ UK and European patent matters. US patent attorneys and agents, and colleagues worldwide, are welcome, whether you need help with a single application or ongoing European prosecution.

Your work is handled personally by Dr Ritchie Lewis Daniel, a Chartered Patent Attorney and European Patent Attorney based in Bath, United Kingdom. You have direct access to the professional representative responsible for the EPO work.

Direct collaboration

Discuss the application, prosecution options and commercial priorities directly with the attorney doing the work.

Your client relationship

Routine instructions and reporting are handled through you. Any direct contact with your client is agreed with you, subject to professional obligations.

Clear scope and costs

The scope, charging basis and reporting arrangements are agreed at the outset. Additional substantive work is discussed with you before proceeding.

UK AND EUROPEAN PATENTS

Support where you need it.

European patent filings

Direct European applications and PCT European regional-phase entry, with the filing requirements and scope of any application review agreed in advance.

EPO prosecution

Review of search opinions and examination communications, practical advice on response options, and preparation and filing of responses on your instructions.

Existing applications

Taking over European representation, subject to conflict checks, review of the file and confirmation of outstanding deadlines.

UK patent matters

UK patent applications and prosecution before the UKIPO, alongside advice on the UK and European filing strategy for your client.

TECHNICAL UNDERSTANDING

An engineer’s perspective.

Ritchie holds a PhD and MEng in Mechanical Engineering. His experience spans private practice, hands-on automotive engineering and in-house patent work within product development.

That background is particularly relevant to mechanical inventions, consumer products, manufacturing and related engineering technologies. Share a high-level description of the subject matter so that technical fit can be assessed before accepting instructions.

Advice you can use.

Reporting is tailored to the scope you need: forwarding an official communication, providing a reasoned recommendation, or preparing a proposed response. The aim is to help you explain the available options and their implications to your client.

For each matter, we agree who gives instructions, who receives correspondence, the billing arrangements and how deadlines will be confirmed. Estimates distinguish professional fees from official fees and other disbursements.
