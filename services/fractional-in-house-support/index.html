WHAT FRACTIONAL SUPPORT MEANS

Continuity across IP decisions.

Fractional in-house patent counsel is an external patent attorney working closely with a business to provide some of the capability of an internal IP role. The value comes from understanding the products, people and priorities well enough to help as decisions arise.

Dr Ritchie Daniel can work alongside founders, leadership, engineering, product and R&D teams, as well as legal teams and existing external advisers. Regular invention discussions, product reviews and portfolio decisions can help turn separate patent instructions into a coherent plan.

Which businesses benefit?

The model can suit a startup preparing for a first product launch or investment, a scale-up developing several technologies, or an established SME with an active engineering team and no dedicated patent function. An existing IP team may also need additional capacity for a defined programme or period of growth.

For a single filing or isolated question, a patent instruction or defined piece of IP advice may be sufficient. Fractional support becomes useful when continuity and knowledge of the business improve the decisions.

Practical support across products and portfolios.

Invention capture and prioritisation

Strategy, portfolios and budgets

Coordinate instructions to external patent attorneys and foreign associates, bring business context to their work and review reporting, costs and next steps.

Plan freedom-to-operate work, consider relevant competitor patents and help engineering and leadership teams understand product risks, design alternatives and where further analysis is needed.
