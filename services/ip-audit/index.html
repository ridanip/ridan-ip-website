SCOPE

A practical view of the IP position.

An IP audit can help a business understand which rights and information matter, whether ownership and protection arrangements are in order, where material gaps or risks may exist, and which actions deserve priority.

  • Identify relevant patents, designs, trade marks, copyright, trade secrets and know-how
  • Review ownership, assignments, licences and contractual arrangements
  • Consider protection gaps, disclosure risks and third-party rights
  • Align IP activity with products, commercial plans and available budget
  • Produce clear findings and prioritised next steps
