Prepare a clearer IP position.

The checklist is intended for founders, business leaders and investors preparing an engineering or hardware business for funding. You can complete the fields on screen or print a copy.

  • Record which evidence you have and where the uncertainties remain.
  • Connect an important product to relevant rights and the scope of any freedom-to-operate advice.
  • Organise due diligence documents and assign practical actions before the next commitment.

The checklist helps you prepare for a review. Completing the fields does not establish ownership, patent coverage, validity or freedom to operate.

Requests are reviewed personally by Ritchie Daniel for business relevance. If approved, the PDF will be emailed to you. Access is subject to approval, and requesting a copy does not create an attorney-client relationship.
