Fractional in-house IP support. When does it make sense?

Practical guidance for startups, SMEs and growing technology businesses considering experienced intellectual property support on a flexible basis.

What is fractional in-house IP support?

Fractional support provides regular access to experienced intellectual property expertise without requiring a dedicated internal IP function.

When businesses typically consider it

Some businesses reach a stage where intellectual property decisions become more frequent, while the requirement may not justify a full internal team.

Supporting startups and scale-ups

Support may include patent strategy, disclosure management, investor discussions and coordination with external advisers.

Supporting SMEs

Established businesses may use fractional support to manage innovation programmes, supplier relationships and portfolio decisions.

Bridging the gap before a full-time hire

The business may benefit from experienced IP support on a flexible basis while requirements continue to develop.

Working alongside management and engineering teams

Fractional support can help connect commercial objectives, product development and intellectual property decisions.

Investment and due diligence support

Support may include ownership reviews, portfolio summaries and responding to diligence requests.

When it may not be appropriate

Some organisations may already have sufficient internal capability or may require a dedicated specialist team.

Frequently asked questions

Is fractional support only for startups? No. SMEs, scale-ups and technology businesses may also find it useful.

Does it replace external patent attorneys? Not necessarily. It may complement existing advisers.

Conclusion

The strongest arrangements are usually those that align intellectual property support with the stage, priorities and resources of the business.
