Accessibility Statement

RIDAN IP is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as reasonably possible.

Commitment

RIDAN IP aims to provide a clear and consistent website experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices. Content is intended to be straightforward, readable and accessible to a wide range of users and technologies.

Accessibility features

Current accessibility features include:

  • consistent navigation throughout the website;
  • responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile devices;
  • keyboard-accessible navigation, links and controls;
  • a skip-to-content link;
  • alternative text for important images; and
  • colour choices intended to maintain contrast between text and backgrounds.

Browser and device compatibility

The website is intended to work with current versions of major web browsers and modern assistive technologies. Performance may vary on older devices, browsers or software that are no longer supported by their developers.

Feedback and assistance

RIDAN IP welcomes feedback on the accessibility of this website.

If any content is difficult to access, information is required in an alternative format, or an accessibility issue is identified, please contact RIDAN IP:

Email: info@ridanip.com

RIDAN IP will make reasonable efforts to provide information in an accessible format and to address reported issues where practicable.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is considered as the website develops and content is updated. RIDAN IP reviews the website periodically and may make further improvements to enhance accessibility and usability.
