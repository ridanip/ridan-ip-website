Protecting creative works.

Copyright may protect original works such as software code, technical drawings, written content and graphical material.

COPYRIGHT

Key considerations.

01

What copyright protects

Original literary, artistic, musical and dramatic works and other protected subject matter.

02

Automatic protection

UK copyright generally arises automatically without a registration application.

03

Software and drawings

Software code, technical drawings and graphical material may fall within copyright protection.

04

Ownership

Employment, commissioning and contractor arrangements can affect who owns the copyright.

05

Evidence

Records of creation, authorship, dates and transfers can be important in practice.

06

Commercial use

Copyright may be assigned or licensed as part of broader commercial arrangements.

This page provides general information rather than advice on any particular circumstances. The appropriate protection depends on the relevant subject matter, disclosures, territories and commercial objectives.
