IPRs / REGISTERED DESIGNS

Protecting product appearance.

Registered designs can protect the appearance of a product, including features such as shape, configuration, pattern and ornamentation.

REGISTERED DESIGNS

Key considerations.

01

What designs protect

The visual appearance of a product rather than how the product works.

02

Suitable features

Shape, configuration, pattern, ornamentation and combinations of visual features may be relevant.

03

Novelty

Public disclosure before filing can affect the protection available and should be considered carefully.

04

Representations

The images filed with an application help define the appearance for which protection is sought.

05

Multiple designs

A filing strategy may cover variants, components and alternative product appearances.

06

Commercial alignment

Protection should focus on designs that matter to product identity and competitive advantage.

This page provides general information rather than advice on any particular circumstances. The appropriate protection depends on the relevant subject matter, disclosures, territories and commercial objectives.
