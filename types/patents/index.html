PATENTS

Key considerations.

01

What patents protect

Technical features defining how a product, system or process works.

02

Patentability

Novelty, inventive step and industrial applicability are central considerations.

03

Applications and publication

Protection is pursued through applications that generally publish the technical teaching.

04

Territorial protection

Rights are obtained for selected countries or regions rather than automatically worldwide.

05

Commercial alignment

Filing choices should reflect products, markets, competitors and business objectives.

06

Professional support

Patent drafting and prosecution require careful technical and legal judgement.

This page provides general information rather than advice on any particular circumstances. The appropriate protection depends on the relevant subject matter, disclosures, territories and commercial objectives.
