Direct access to experienced advice.

RIDAN IP was established to give innovative businesses direct access to experienced intellectual property advice without layers of hierarchy or administration.

Whether the challenge is protecting innovation, assessing risk, managing a patent portfolio or obtaining flexible in-house support, the objective is the same: helping clients make informed decisions with confidence.

I am a Chartered Patent Attorney, European Patent Attorney and Unified Patent Court Representative with fifteen years' experience in the profession, including more than ten years' post-qualification and over three years in-house at Dyson.

I draft and prosecute patents, advise on UK and European patent strategy, freedom to operate and product clearance, registered designs, trade secrets, IP opinions and due diligence, and provide fractional in-house support.

Clients work directly with me, from the initial discussion and development of the strategy through to delivery of the advice and work.
